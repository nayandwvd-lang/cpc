# Courtroom Drafting Toolkit

Model petitions and applications, drawn to the standard a District Court or High Court registry will accept, with the statutory hooks made explicit. Every draft here is a skeleton to be filled, not a form to be filed unread — the facts, the valuation, the court fee and the State amendment are yours to check.

::: warning How to use these drafts
Each model is annotated with the provision it rests on and the failure mode it is drafted to avoid. Placeholders appear as `____`. Before filing, verify: (i) the pecuniary and territorial jurisdiction of the court named; (ii) the court fee under the applicable **State** Court Fees Act; (iii) the current text of the Rule as amended in your State; (iv) whether the matter is a commercial dispute of a Specified Value, which changes the procedural regime entirely; and (v) the limitation article.
:::

## The universal skeleton

Every civil pleading and application in India shares a common frame. Learn it once and the rest is content.

| Element | Content | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| **Cause title** | Court, case number, and the parties described by name, parentage, age, occupation and address | O. VII R. 1(a)–(d) |
| **Heading** | "Application under [provision] read with Section 151 CPC" — the provision first, s. 151 as residue | S. 151 |
| **Body** | Numbered paragraphs, one fact each, chronological, material facts only | O. VI R. 2 |
| **Grounds** | Where the application is contested law, a separate lettered list of grounds | — |
| **Prayer** | Lettered clauses, each self-contained and capable of being read as an order | O. VII R. 7 |
| **Verification** | Paragraph-wise, distinguishing knowledge from information and belief, with the source of information stated | O. VI R. 15 |
| **Affidavit** | Supporting affidavit; in commercial suits, a Statement of Truth | S. 26(2); O. VI R. 15A |
| **Schedule** | Property described by boundaries and survey/settlement numbers | O. VII R. 3 |
| **List of documents** | With the documents produced | O. VII R. 14; O. VIII R. 1A |

::: tip The prayer test
Read each prayer clause aloud, imagining it copied verbatim into an order and handed to a bailiff. If the bailiff cannot tell exactly what to do, redraft it. "Restraining the Defendants from interfering with the Plaintiff's rights" fails this test; "restraining the Defendants from entering upon the property described in Schedule A" passes it.
:::

## 1. Plaint — suit for permanent injunction and declaration

**Rests on:** Order VII Rules 1–7; Section 34, Specific Relief Act, 1963
**Drafted to avoid:** rejection under Order VII Rule 11(a) and (d)

> **IN THE COURT OF THE ____ CIVIL JUDGE (SENIOR DIVISION) AT ____**
>
> **CIVIL SUIT No. ____ of 20__**
>
> **A B**, son of ____, aged ____ years, occupation ____, resident of ____ … **PLAINTIFF**
>
> **VERSUS**
>
> **C D**, son of ____, aged ____ years, occupation ____, resident of ____ … **DEFENDANT**
>
> **SUIT FOR DECLARATION OF TITLE, POSSESSION AND PERMANENT INJUNCTION**
>
> The Plaintiff above named most respectfully submits as under:
>
> **1. Description of the parties.** The Plaintiff is the owner in possession of the immovable property more particularly described in Schedule A hereto (hereinafter "the suit property"). The Defendant is the owner of the adjoining property described in Schedule B.
>
> **2. Title.** The Plaintiff purchased the suit property from one ____ by a registered sale deed dated ____, registered as Document No. ____ of ____ in the office of the Sub-Registrar, ____. A certified copy is filed herewith as Document No. 1 in the List of Documents. The Plaintiff's name stands mutated in the revenue records vide Mutation Entry No. ____ dated ____.
>
> **3. Possession.** Since the date of purchase the Plaintiff has been in continuous, peaceful and exclusive possession and enjoyment of the suit property, paying all taxes and outgoings in respect thereof, particulars whereof are set out in Schedule C.
>
> **4. The cause of action — the threatened act.** On ____ at about ____ hours the Defendant, accompanied by ____ persons, entered upon the suit property and attempted to demolish the boundary wall on the ____ side, asserting that the strip of land measuring ____ described in Schedule D forms part of the Defendant's property.
>
> **5.** On ____ the Plaintiff caused a legal notice to be issued to the Defendant through his advocate, calling upon the Defendant to desist. The said notice was duly served upon the Defendant on ____. The Defendant sent a reply dated ____ denying the Plaintiff's title and asserting his own. Copies of the notice, the acknowledgement and the reply are filed as Documents Nos. ____ to ____.
>
> **6. The necessity for a declaration.** By reason of the Defendant's assertion of title, a cloud has been cast upon the Plaintiff's title, and the Plaintiff is entitled to a declaration that he is the absolute owner of the suit property, together with the consequential reliefs of possession of the strip described in Schedule D and a permanent injunction.
>
> **7. Cause of action.** The cause of action for the suit first arose on ____, when the Defendant entered upon the suit property and asserted title; it arose again on ____ when the Defendant sent his reply denying the Plaintiff's title; and it is a continuing one, the Defendant continuing to threaten the Plaintiff's possession, all within the territorial jurisdiction of this Hon'ble Court.
>
> **8. Jurisdiction.** The suit property is situate within the territorial limits of the jurisdiction of this Hon'ble Court. The value of the subject matter being ₹____, the suit is within the pecuniary jurisdiction of this Hon'ble Court. The suit is of a civil nature and the cognizance thereof is not barred, expressly or impliedly, by any statute.
>
> **9. Limitation.** The suit is within the period of limitation prescribed by Article ____ of the Schedule to the Limitation Act, 1963.
>
> **10. Valuation and court fee.** For the purposes of jurisdiction the suit is valued at ₹____, being the market value of the suit property. For the purposes of court fee: (a) the relief of declaration is valued at ₹____ and a court fee of ₹____ is paid thereon under Section ____ of the ____ Court Fees Act; (b) the relief of possession is valued at ₹____ and an ad valorem court fee of ₹____ is paid thereon; and (c) the relief of permanent injunction is valued at ₹____ and a court fee of ₹____ is paid thereon. The total court fee of ₹____ is paid on this plaint.
>
> **11.** The Plaintiff has not filed any other suit or proceeding in respect of the subject matter of this suit before any court or tribunal.
>
> **PRAYER**
>
> The Plaintiff therefore prays that this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to:
>
> **(a)** Declare that the Plaintiff is the absolute owner of the immovable property described in Schedule A hereto;
>
> **(b)** Direct the Defendant to deliver vacant and peaceful possession of the strip of land described in Schedule D hereto to the Plaintiff;
>
> **(c)** Grant a decree of permanent injunction restraining the Defendant, his servants, agents and all persons claiming through or under him, from entering upon the suit property described in Schedule A, from demolishing or interfering with the boundary wall thereon, and from in any manner disturbing the Plaintiff's peaceful possession and enjoyment thereof;
>
> **(d)** Direct an enquiry into mesne profits under Order XX Rule 12 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 in respect of the strip described in Schedule D from ____ until the date of delivery of possession, and decree the amount so ascertained;
>
> **(e)** Award the costs of the suit to the Plaintiff;
>
> **(f)** Pass such other and further orders as this Hon'ble Court may deem fit and proper in the facts and circumstances of the case, and in the interests of justice.
>
> **VERIFICATION**
>
> I, A B, the Plaintiff above named, do hereby verify that the contents of paragraphs 1 to 3 and 7 to 11 of the above plaint are true to my personal knowledge, and that the contents of paragraphs 4 to 6 are true to my information derived from ____ and believed by me to be true, and that I have not suppressed any material fact. Verified at ____ on this ____ day of ____, 20__.

::: danger The four things that get this plaint rejected
1. **No date of accrual in paragraph 7.** Order VII Rule 1(e) requires the cause of action "and when it arose". Its absence is the standard route to a Rule 11(d) limitation objection.
2. **Declaration without consequential relief.** The proviso to Section 34 of the Specific Relief Act, 1963 bars a bare declaration where the plaintiff is able to seek further relief. If you are out of possession, you must claim possession.
3. **Schedule that does not identify the property.** Order VII Rule 3 requires boundaries or survey numbers. A decree on an unidentifiable property is unexecutable.
4. **Court fee stated as a lump sum.** Value each relief separately and state the provision under which each is valued.
:::

## 2. Application for temporary injunction

**Rests on:** Order XXXIX Rules 1 and 2 read with Section 151
**Drafted to avoid:** refusal for want of the three principles, and vacation under the first proviso to Rule 4

> **IN THE COURT OF ____ AT ____**
>
> **I.A. No. ____ of 20__**
> **IN**
> **CIVIL SUIT No. ____ of 20__**
>
> **APPLICATION UNDER ORDER XXXIX RULES 1 AND 2 READ WITH SECTION 151 OF THE CODE OF CIVIL PROCEDURE, 1908**
>
> The Applicant/Plaintiff most respectfully submits:
>
> **1.** The Applicant has this day instituted the accompanying suit against the Respondent/Defendant for declaration, possession and permanent injunction in respect of the property described in Schedule A to the plaint. The contents of the plaint are not repeated for the sake of brevity and may kindly be read as part and parcel of this application.
>
> **2. The Applicant's right.** The Applicant is the owner in possession of the suit property under a registered sale deed dated ____, and his name stands mutated in the revenue records. Copies are annexed as Annexures P-1 and P-2.
>
> **3. The threatened act.** On ____ the Respondent entered upon the suit property with labourers and building material and commenced excavation for a foundation upon the strip described in Schedule D. Photographs taken on ____, bearing the date stamp, are annexed as Annexure P-3.
>
> **4.** Unless restrained, the Respondent will complete the construction within a matter of weeks, thereby altering the nature and character of the suit property and presenting this Hon'ble Court with a fait accompli.
>
> **5. Prima facie case.** The Applicant has a strong prima facie case, as appears from the registered sale deed (Annexure P-1), the mutation entry (Annexure P-2) and the tax receipts (Annexure P-4), all of which establish the Applicant's title to and possession of the suit property. The Respondent has produced no document of title in his reply notice dated ____.
>
> **6. Balance of convenience.** The balance of convenience lies wholly in favour of the Applicant. If the injunction is granted, the Respondent suffers no more than a postponement of construction upon land to which he has shown no title. If it is refused, the Applicant will lose the strip permanently in substance, and will be relegated to a suit for demolition which will take years and which, even if successful, will not restore the Applicant's boundary wall or the character of his property.
>
> **7. Irreparable injury.** The injury threatened is irreparable and incapable of being compensated in money. The suit property is immovable property of a special character, being the Applicant's ancestral residence, and no award of damages can restore it. Further, the Respondent's means are not such as would satisfy a decree for damages.
>
> **8. Urgency and the necessity for an ex parte order.** The Respondent has engaged labour and commenced excavation, and the construction is proceeding daily. If notice is issued and the matter is heard after service, the construction will be complete before the first effective hearing, and the very object of granting the injunction will be defeated by the delay. It is therefore necessary that ad interim ex parte relief be granted, and the Applicant undertakes to comply forthwith with the requirements of the proviso to Order XXXIX Rule 3.
>
> **9. Disclosure.** In discharge of his duty of full and frank disclosure, the Applicant states that: (i) a notice dated ____ was received by the Applicant from the Respondent, a copy of which is annexed as Annexure P-5; (ii) proceedings under Section ____ are pending between the parties before the ____ Court, being Case No. ____, in which an order dated ____ was passed, a copy of which is annexed as Annexure P-6; and (iii) save as aforesaid, the Applicant has not instituted and there is not pending any other proceeding in respect of the subject matter of this suit before any court or tribunal.
>
> **10. Undertaking.** The Applicant undertakes to abide by any order that this Hon'ble Court may make as to damages, in case this Hon'ble Court shall hereafter be of the opinion that the Respondent has sustained any by reason of this order which the Applicant ought to pay; and the Applicant is willing to furnish such security as this Hon'ble Court may direct.
>
> **PRAYER**
>
> It is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to:
>
> **(a)** Grant an ad interim ex parte temporary injunction restraining the Respondent, his servants, agents, contractors, labourers and all persons claiming through or under him, from raising any construction upon, excavating, or in any manner altering the nature and character of the strip of land described in Schedule D hereto, beyond the position existing as on the date of this application;
>
> **(b)** Grant an ad interim ex parte temporary injunction restraining the Respondent from demolishing or interfering with the boundary wall standing on the ____ side of the property described in Schedule A hereto, and from dispossessing the Applicant therefrom otherwise than in due process of law;
>
> **(c)** Appoint a Local Commissioner under Order XXVI Rule 9 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 to visit the suit property, to measure the same with the assistance of a qualified surveyor with reference to the revenue records, and to report upon the physical condition of the property, the nature and extent of the construction thereon, and the person in actual physical possession;
>
> **(d)** Confirm the ad interim orders aforesaid after notice to the Respondent, and continue the same until the disposal of the suit;
>
> **(e)** Award the costs of this application to the Applicant;
>
> **(f)** Pass such other and further orders as this Hon'ble Court may deem fit and proper in the interests of justice.

::: danger Order XXXIX Rule 3 — the compliance step that is always forgotten
If ex parte relief is granted, the proviso to Rule 3 obliges the applicant to (a) deliver or send by registered post to the opposite party, **immediately**, a copy of the application, the supporting affidavit, the plaint and the documents relied on; and (b) **file an affidavit** stating that this has been done, on the day the injunction is granted or the day immediately following. Failure to file the compliance affidavit is an independent ground for vacation, irrespective of the merits. Prepare the compliance affidavit before you leave the court building.
:::

## 3. Written statement with counter-claim

**Rests on:** Order VIII Rules 1–5, 6A–6G
**Drafted to avoid:** deemed admission under Rule 5(1) and forfeiture under the Rule 1 proviso

> **IN THE COURT OF ____ AT ____**
>
> **CIVIL SUIT No. ____ of 20__**
>
> **WRITTEN STATEMENT ON BEHALF OF THE DEFENDANT, AND COUNTER-CLAIM UNDER ORDER VIII RULE 6A OF THE CODE OF CIVIL PROCEDURE, 1908**
>
> The Defendant above named most respectfully submits:
>
> **PART A — PRELIMINARY OBJECTIONS**
>
> **1.** The suit is barred by limitation. On the Plaintiff's own showing in paragraph ____ of the plaint, the cause of action arose on ____, and the suit having been instituted on ____ is beyond the period of ____ years prescribed by Article ____ of the Schedule to the Limitation Act, 1963. No ground of exemption is pleaded as required by Order VII Rule 6, and the plaint is liable to be rejected under Order VII Rule 11(d).
>
> **2.** The suit is barred by the principle of res judicata under Section 11 of the Code. The matter directly and substantially in issue in the present suit was directly and substantially in issue in Suit No. ____ of ____ on the file of the ____ Court, between the same parties, which was heard and finally decided by judgment and decree dated ____. A certified copy of the judgment and decree is filed herewith.
>
> **3.** The suit is bad for non-joinder of a necessary party, in that ____, in whose favour the Plaintiff has executed an agreement to sell dated ____ in respect of the suit property, has not been impleaded, and no effective decree can be passed in his absence. This objection is taken at the earliest possible opportunity as required by Order I Rule 13.
>
> **4.** The plaint is undervalued and the court fee paid is deficient. The market value of the suit property is not less than ₹____, and ad valorem court fee is payable thereon.
>
> **PART B — PARAWISE REPLY**
>
> **5.** With reference to paragraph 1 of the plaint, the description of the parties is admitted. The description of the suit property is denied. It is specifically denied that the Plaintiff is the owner in possession of the property described in Schedule A to the plaint, or of any part thereof.
>
> **6.** With reference to paragraph 2 of the plaint, the execution and registration of the sale deed dated ____ is admitted, but it is specifically denied that the said sale deed conveyed any right, title or interest in the strip described in Schedule D, the vendor thereunder having had no title thereto. The mutation entry is admitted, but it is submitted that mutation entries are for fiscal purposes and do not confer title.
>
> **7.** With reference to paragraph 3 of the plaint, it is specifically denied that the Plaintiff has been in continuous, peaceful or exclusive possession of the strip described in Schedule D, or of any part thereof, at any time. The Defendant has been in possession thereof since ____.
>
> **8.** With reference to paragraph 4 of the plaint, it is denied that the Defendant entered upon the suit property or attempted to demolish any boundary wall on ____ or on any other date, or that the Defendant was accompanied by any persons as alleged or at all. The Defendant has at all material times carried out work upon his own property.
>
> *[Continue, one paragraph of the written statement to each paragraph of the plaint, to the end. Leave no gap.]*
>
> **PART C — THE DEFENDANT'S CASE**
>
> **__.** The true facts are these. *[Set out the Defendant's own version, with dates, affirmatively.]*
>
> **PART D — COUNTER-CLAIM**
>
> **__.** The Defendant, in addition to his defence aforesaid, **sets up the following claim by way of counter-claim** under Order VIII Rule 6A of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 against the Plaintiff. The cause of action for this counter-claim accrued to the Defendant on ____, that is to say, before the delivery of this defence.
>
> **__.** *[Plead the counter-claim as a plaint: the Defendant's title, the Plaintiff's wrongful act, the cause of action and its date, jurisdiction, limitation, valuation and court fee.]*
>
> **__.** The counter-claim is valued at ₹____ for the purposes of jurisdiction and court fee, and a court fee of ₹____ is paid thereon as on a plaint, in accordance with Order VIII Rule 6A(4). The counter-claim does not exceed the pecuniary limits of the jurisdiction of this Hon'ble Court.
>
> **PRAYER**
>
> The Defendant therefore prays that this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to:
>
> **(a)** Dismiss the suit of the Plaintiff with costs;
>
> **(b)** Decree the counter-claim of the Defendant and declare that the Defendant is the absolute owner of the strip of land described in Schedule D hereto;
>
> **(c)** Direct the Plaintiff to deliver vacant and peaceful possession of the said strip to the Defendant;
>
> **(d)** Direct an enquiry into mesne profits under Order XX Rule 12 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 from the date of filing of this counter-claim until delivery of possession, and decree the amount so ascertained;
>
> **(e)** Grant a permanent injunction restraining the Plaintiff from interfering with the Defendant's possession of the said strip;
>
> **(f)** Award the costs of the suit and of the counter-claim to the Defendant;
>
> **(g)** Pass such other and further orders as this Hon'ble Court may deem fit in the interests of justice.

::: danger Three sentences a defendant cannot get wrong
1. **"Service was effected on ____; the 120-day period expires on ____."** In a commercial suit of a Specified Value the right to file is **forfeited** on expiry and the court has no power to take the written statement on record.
2. **"With reference to paragraph ____ of the plaint…"** — for every paragraph. Anything not specifically denied, denied by necessary implication, or stated to be not admitted is **deemed admitted** under Rule 5(1), and exposes you to judgment on admissions under Order XII Rule 6.
3. **"The Defendant sets up the following claim by way of counter-claim."** Without those words, Rule 6B is not satisfied and there is no counter-claim — only a defence, on which no decree can be passed in your favour.
:::

## 4. Application for rejection of plaint

**Rests on:** Order VII Rule 11
**Drafted to avoid:** the four ways a Rule 11 application is lost

> **APPLICATION UNDER ORDER VII RULE 11 OF THE CODE OF CIVIL PROCEDURE, 1908**
>
> The Applicant/Defendant most respectfully submits:
>
> **1.** The Plaintiff has instituted the present suit for ____. The Applicant submits that the plaint is liable to be rejected at the threshold, and that the Applicant ought not to be put to the expense and vexation of a trial which can have only one outcome.
>
> **2.** It is settled that an application under Order VII Rule 11 falls to be decided **on the averments in the plaint alone**, taking every averment therein as true, and without reference to the defence. The Applicant accordingly confines this application to the four corners of the plaint and the documents filed therewith.
>
> **GROUNDS**
>
> **A. The plaint discloses no cause of action — Rule 11(a).**
> The plaint pleads, in paragraphs ____ to ____, only conclusions of law and epithets. It nowhere pleads (i) ____; (ii) ____; or (iii) ____, each of which is a material fact which the Plaintiff must prove in order to obtain the relief claimed. Assuming every averment in the plaint to be true, no decree could be passed. The plaint therefore fails Order VI Rule 2 and is liable to be rejected under Order VII Rule 11(a).
>
> **B. The suit appears from the statement in the plaint to be barred by law — Rule 11(d).**
> **(i) Limitation.** Paragraph ____ of the plaint states that the cause of action arose on ____. The suit was instituted on ____, beyond the period of ____ years prescribed by Article ____ of the Schedule to the Limitation Act, 1963. No ground of exemption is pleaded, as Order VII Rule 6 mandatorily requires. The bar is apparent from the statement in the plaint and requires no evidence.
> **(ii) Specific Relief Act.** The plaint seeks a bare declaration in paragraph ____ of the prayer, while paragraph ____ of the plaint itself pleads that the Plaintiff was dispossessed on ____. The Plaintiff being able to seek the further relief of possession and having omitted to do so, the proviso to Section 34 of the Specific Relief Act, 1963 bars the declaration.
> **(iii) Statutory bar.** The suit is in respect of a matter in respect of which the jurisdiction of the civil court is expressly barred by Section ____ of the ____ Act, which provides a complete code and an exclusive forum.
>
> **PRAYER**
>
> It is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to:
>
> **(a)** Reject the plaint in Civil Suit No. ____ of 20__ under Order VII Rule 11(a) and (d) of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908;
>
> **(b)** Pending the hearing and final disposal of this application, stay all further proceedings in the suit, including the framing of issues and the recording of evidence;
>
> **(c)** Award the costs of this application to the Applicant;
>
> **(d)** Pass such other and further orders as this Hon'ble Court may deem fit in the interests of justice.

::: warning What not to put in this application
Do not refer to your written statement, your documents, or your version of the facts. The moment the argument requires the judge to look outside the plaint, the application fails. Do not ask for rejection "as against Defendant No. 3" — the plaint is rejected as a whole or not at all; use Order I Rule 10(2) instead. Do not raise res judicata or the Order II Rule 2 bar here unless the entire foundation appears on the plaint itself — both normally require the earlier record and belong to a preliminary issue under Order XIV Rule 2.
:::

## 5. Application for leave to amend a pleading

**Rests on:** Order VI Rule 17 read with Section 151
**Drafted to avoid:** the proviso, and the lapse of leave under Rule 18

> **APPLICATION UNDER ORDER VI RULE 17 READ WITH SECTION 151 OF THE CODE OF CIVIL PROCEDURE, 1908 FOR LEAVE TO AMEND THE PLAINT**
>
> **1.** The Applicant/Plaintiff seeks leave to amend the plaint in the manner set out in the Schedule of Amendments annexed hereto and marked **Annexure A-1**.
>
> **2. The necessity for the amendment.** In his written statement dated ____ the Defendant has, for the first time, asserted title to the suit property and denied the Applicant's possession. The real question in controversy between the parties is therefore the title to the suit property, and unless the amendment is allowed the suit as framed will not determine that question, and the Applicant will be driven to a second suit, resulting in multiplicity of proceedings.
>
> **3. No change in the nature of the suit.** The proposed amendment does not introduce a new or inconsistent cause of action. It seeks only to add the reliefs of declaration and, in the alternative, possession, founded on the very facts already pleaded in paragraphs ____ to ____ of the plaint.
>
> **4. No accrued right is disturbed.** The proposed amendment does not deprive the Defendant of any right accrued to him by lapse of time. The claim for possession is founded on the Applicant's dispossession on ____ and is well within the period of twelve years prescribed by Article ____ of the Schedule to the Limitation Act, 1963.
>
> **5. Stage of the proceedings.** Trial has not commenced. Issues have not been framed, no affidavit of examination-in-chief has been filed under Order XVIII Rule 4, and no evidence has been recorded. The proviso to Order VI Rule 17 is therefore not attracted.
>
> **6. Due diligence** *(include only where trial has commenced)***.** In spite of due diligence, the Applicant could not have raised this matter before the commencement of trial, in that: (i) on ____ the Applicant applied to the office of the Sub-Registrar, ____ for a certified copy of Document No. ____, as appears from the application annexed as Annexure A-2; (ii) the said application was rejected on ____ for the reason that ____; (iii) the Applicant thereafter applied on ____ under the Right to Information Act, 2005, and the document was furnished only on ____, as appears from Annexure A-3. The facts sought to be introduced were not and could not with reasonable diligence have been within the Applicant's knowledge before that date.
>
> **7. Court fee.** The Applicant is ready and willing to pay such additional court fee as may be found payable upon the amended valuation, within such time as this Hon'ble Court may fix.
>
> **PRAYER**
>
> It is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to:
>
> **(a)** Grant leave to the Applicant to amend the plaint in the manner set out in the Schedule of Amendments annexed hereto as Annexure A-1;
>
> **(b)** Permit the Applicant to file the amended plaint within such time as this Hon'ble Court may fix, and to pay the additional court fee found payable thereon;
>
> **(c)** Grant liberty to the Defendant to file an additional written statement to the amended plaint within the time prescribed by Order VIII Rule 1;
>
> **(d)** Pass such other and further orders as this Hon'ble Court may deem fit in the interests of justice.

**Annexure A-1 — Schedule of Amendments**

| Para | Existing text | Proposed text | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | *[reproduce verbatim]* | *[reproduce verbatim, with additions underlined]* | To plead the Applicant's title, the Defendant having denied it in paragraph ____ of the written statement |
| Prayer (a) | — | *[new prayer clause in full]* | To claim the consequential relief of possession, without which a declaration is barred by the proviso to Section 34 of the Specific Relief Act, 1963 |

::: danger Order VI Rule 18 — diarise the day leave is granted
Where leave to amend is granted, the amendment must be carried out within the time fixed by the court, or, if no time is fixed, **within fourteen days** of the order. If it is not, the party may not amend except with fresh leave. Applications are won and then lost on this rule. Enter the date in the file the moment the order is passed.
:::

## 6. Application for restitution

**Rests on:** Section 144 read with Section 151
**Drafted to avoid:** filing in the wrong court, and omitting the consequential heads

> **APPLICATION UNDER SECTION 144 READ WITH SECTION 151 OF THE CODE OF CIVIL PROCEDURE, 1908 FOR RESTITUTION**
>
> *[To be filed in the **Court of First Instance**, by force of Explanation (a) to Section 144(1), even where the reversal was by the High Court or the Supreme Court.]*
>
> **1.** The Applicant was the Defendant in Civil Suit No. ____ of 20__ on the file of this Hon'ble Court, in which a decree was passed against him on ____ *[describe the decree]*.
>
> **2.** In execution of the said decree, in Execution Petition No. ____ of 20__, (i) a sum of ₹____ was recovered from the Applicant on ____ by attachment of his account with ____ Bank and paid out to the Respondent; and (ii) possession of the property described in Schedule A hereto was delivered to the Respondent on ____.
>
> **3.** By judgment and decree dated ____ passed in First Appeal No. ____ of 20__, the Hon'ble ____ Court was pleased to allow the appeal of the Applicant, set aside the decree dated ____ and dismiss the suit. A certified copy is annexed as Annexure A-1.
>
> **4.** By reason of the reversal of the said decree, the Applicant is entitled, as of right, to be placed in the position which he would have occupied but for the said decree. The obligation to make restitution arises automatically upon the reversal, and it is the duty of this Hon'ble Court, being the court which passed the decree, to cause such restitution to be made.
>
> **5. Limitation.** This application is made within twelve years of the date on which the decree dated ____ became enforceable, and is within time under Article 136 of the Schedule to the Limitation Act, 1963.
>
> **6. Quantification.**
>
> | Head | Amount / relief | Basis |
> |---|---|---|
> | Principal recovered | ₹____ | Paid out on ____ per the execution record |
> | Interest | ____ % p.a. from ____ to date of refund | Being the Applicant's actual cost of borrowing, per Annexure A-2 |
> | Costs paid under the reversed decree | ₹____ | Per the decree |
> | Possession | Property in Schedule A | Delivered to the Respondent on ____ |
> | Mesne profits | To be ascertained on enquiry | Letting value per Annexure A-3 |
>
> **PRAYER**
>
> It is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to:
>
> **(a)** Direct the Respondent to restore to the Applicant vacant and peaceful possession of the property described in Schedule A hereto;
>
> **(b)** Direct the Respondent to refund to the Applicant the sum of ₹____, together with interest thereon at the rate of ____ per cent per annum from ____, being the date of recovery, until payment;
>
> **(c)** Direct the refund of the costs of ₹____ paid by the Applicant under the reversed decree;
>
> **(d)** Direct an enquiry into the mesne profits payable by the Respondent in respect of the said property for the period from ____ until restoration of possession, and decree the amount so ascertained in favour of the Applicant;
>
> **(e)** Pending the hearing and final disposal of this application, restrain the Respondent from alienating, transferring, or creating any third-party right, title or interest in the property described in Schedule A;
>
> **(f)** Award the costs of this application to the Applicant;
>
> **(g)** Pass such other and further orders as this Hon'ble Court may deem fit in the interests of justice.

::: warning Two traps in restitution
**File the application, not a suit.** Section 144(2) bars a suit for relief obtainable under sub-section (1), and the plaint will be rejected under Order VII Rule 11(d).

**Check who bought at the auction.** If the property was sold in execution to a **bona fide stranger** and the sale has been confirmed, his title survives the reversal and your restitution against the decree-holder is limited to the sale proceeds with interest. If the **decree-holder himself** purchased, he bought subject to the appeal and restitution of the property may be ordered.
:::

## 7. Caveat

**Rests on:** Section 148A
**Drafted to avoid:** the two failures — non-service under sub-section (2), and expiry at ninety days

> **IN THE COURT OF ____ AT ____**
>
> **CAVEAT PETITION No. ____ of 20__**
>
> **UNDER SECTION 148A OF THE CODE OF CIVIL PROCEDURE, 1908**
>
> **In the matter of:** An expected suit / appeal / application by **C D** (the expected Applicant) against **A B** (the Caveator), concerning the property described in the Schedule hereto.
>
> **1.** The Caveator is the owner in possession of the property described in the Schedule hereto / is the decree-holder in Civil Suit No. ____ of 20__ decided on ____.
>
> **2.** By a legal notice dated ____, a copy of which is annexed as **Annexure C-1**, the expected Applicant has called upon the Caveator to ____ and has threatened to institute legal proceedings.
>
> **3.** The Caveator apprehends that the expected Applicant will institute a suit or appeal in this Hon'ble Court and will therein apply for an ad interim ex parte order of injunction / stay of execution / appointment of a receiver / attachment before judgment, and that any such order, if made without hearing the Caveator, will occasion grave and irreparable prejudice to the Caveator.
>
> **4.** The Caveator claims a right to appear before this Hon'ble Court on the hearing of any such application.
>
> **5.** Notice of this Caveat has been served upon the expected Applicant by registered post with acknowledgement due on ____, in compliance with Section 148A(2), and the postal receipt and the acknowledgement are annexed as **Annexures C-2 and C-3**.
>
> **PRAYER**
>
> The Caveator therefore prays that no order be passed upon any application made by the expected Applicant in any suit, appeal or proceeding relating to the subject matter aforesaid without notice to the Caveator and without affording the Caveator an opportunity of being heard.

**Accompanying notice to the expected applicant — Section 148A(2)**

> *By Registered Post A.D.*
>
> To: **C D**, ____
>
> **Subject: Notice of Caveat under Section 148A(2) of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908**
>
> Please take notice that my client, A B, has this day lodged a Caveat under Section 148A of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 in the Court of ____ at ____, being Caveat Petition No. ____ of 20__, in respect of any suit, appeal, application or proceeding which you may institute concerning ____. A copy of the Caveat is enclosed.
>
> You are hereby required, under Section 148A(4) of the said Code, forthwith to furnish my client with a copy of any application made by you, and with copies of all papers and documents filed or to be filed by you in support thereof, at my client's expense.

::: danger Ninety days, and every possible court
A caveat ceases to be in force on the expiry of **ninety days** from lodging, unless the anticipated application has been made within that period. **Diarise day 85 and re-lodge.** And where the prospective applicant has a choice of forum, lodge in each court that could entertain the application — a caveat in the District Court gives no protection against an application in the High Court.
:::

## 8. Application for breach of injunction

**Rests on:** Order XXXIX Rule 2A read with Section 151
**Drafted to avoid:** the six reasons Rule 2A applications fail

> **APPLICATION UNDER ORDER XXXIX RULE 2A READ WITH SECTION 151 OF THE CODE OF CIVIL PROCEDURE, 1908**
>
> **1. The order.** By an order dated ____ passed in I.A. No. ____ of 20__ in the above suit, this Hon'ble Court was pleased to restrain the Respondents "____" *[reproduce the operative part of the order verbatim, in quotation marks]*. A certified copy of the said order is annexed as **Annexure A-1**. The said order was in force at all material times and continues in force.
>
> **2. Knowledge.** The said order was served upon the Respondents on ____ by ____, as appears from the affidavit of service dated ____ filed in this Hon'ble Court and from the postal acknowledgement annexed as **Annexure A-2**. Further, counsel for the Respondents was present in Court when the said order was passed, as appears from the order sheet of that date.
>
> **3. The breach.** Notwithstanding the said order and their knowledge thereof, the Respondents have wilfully and deliberately disobeyed the same, in that: (i) on or about ____ the Respondents commenced ____; (ii) by ____ the Respondents had completed ____; and (iii) photographs of the suit property taken on ____ and on ____, bearing date stamps, are annexed as **Annexures A-3 and A-4** and demonstrate the alteration effected in breach.
>
> **4. Wilfulness.** The breach is wilful and deliberate. The Respondents were served on ____ and commenced the offending work on ____, that is to say ____ days thereafter. At no time did the Respondents apply to this Hon'ble Court for modification, variation or vacation of the said order. The Respondents' conduct is a calculated attempt to present this Hon'ble Court with a fait accompli.
>
> **5. Restoration.** The consequences visited by Rule 2A, being attachment and detention, are coercive; but the Applicant further submits that this Hon'ble Court has inherent power under Section 151 to direct that the position obtaining as on the date of the order be restored, upon the principle that no party shall be permitted to benefit from his own disobedience, and that the Court may undo that which has been done in violation of its own order.
>
> **PRAYER**
>
> It is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to:
>
> **(a)** Hold and declare that the Respondents have wilfully disobeyed the order of injunction dated ____ passed in I.A. No. ____ of 20__;
>
> **(b)** Order the attachment of the properties of the Respondents described in Schedule B hereto under Order XXXIX Rule 2A of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908;
>
> **(c)** Order the detention of Respondent No. 1 in civil prison for such term, not exceeding three months, as this Hon'ble Court may deem fit;
>
> **(d)** In exercise of the inherent powers of this Hon'ble Court under Section 151 of the Code, direct the Respondents to restore the suit property to the position in which it stood on ____, being the date of the said order, and in default to authorise the Applicant to carry out such restoration at the cost of the Respondents;
>
> **(e)** Appoint a Local Commissioner under Order XXVI Rule 9 of the Code to inspect the suit property and to report upon the present state thereof;
>
> **(f)** Award the costs of this application to the Applicant;
>
> **(g)** Pass such other and further orders as this Hon'ble Court may deem fit in the interests of justice.

::: warning Rule 2A dies with the injunction
Rule 2A operates only in respect of a **subsisting** order of injunction under Rule 1 or Rule 2. Once the suit is disposed of, the temporary injunction comes to an end and Rule 2A ceases to be available; the remedy for breach of a **decree** of permanent injunction is execution under **Order XXI Rule 32**. And because the consequence is detention, the disobedience must be proved to be **wilful** — which is why an imprecisely worded injunction is unenforceable, and why the operative part of the order must be reproduced verbatim in paragraph 1.
:::

## 9. Application for appointment of a Local Commissioner

**Rests on:** Order XXVI Rule 9
**Why it matters:** it fixes the physical facts on the date of institution, before they can be altered

> **APPLICATION UNDER ORDER XXVI RULE 9 READ WITH SECTION 151 OF THE CODE OF CIVIL PROCEDURE, 1908**
>
> **1.** The suit relates to the immovable property described in Schedule A. A substantial question arising for determination is the identity, extent and boundaries of the said property, the nature and extent of the construction thereon, and the person in actual physical possession thereof.
>
> **2.** These are matters which cannot satisfactorily be determined upon affidavits, and a local investigation is requisite for the purpose of elucidating the matter in dispute.
>
> **3.** The physical condition of the suit property is liable to be altered by the Respondent, and unless a record of its present condition is made forthwith, this Hon'ble Court will be deprived of the means of ascertaining the position as it obtained at the institution of the suit.
>
> **PRAYER**
>
> It is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to:
>
> **(a)** Appoint an Advocate-Commissioner under Order XXVI Rule 9 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 to visit the property described in Schedule A hereto and to submit a report as to: (i) the identity, extent and boundaries thereof, measured with the assistance of a qualified surveyor with reference to the revenue records and the sanctioned plan; (ii) the nature, extent and stage of any construction thereon; (iii) the person found in actual physical possession thereof, and the indicia of such possession; and (iv) the existence, position and condition of the boundary wall on the ____ side;
>
> **(b)** Direct that the Commissioner be at liberty to take photographs and measurements, to be assisted by a qualified surveyor, and to seek police assistance if necessary;
>
> **(c)** Fix the remuneration of the Commissioner and direct the Applicant to deposit the same;
>
> **(d)** Pass such other and further orders as this Hon'ble Court may deem fit in the interests of justice.

::: tip File this on the same day as the plaint
In any dispute about possession, encroachment or construction, a Commissioner's report recording the position on the date of institution is worth more at trial than any number of affidavits, and it forecloses the "it was always like that" defence. It is the single most underused application in civil practice.
:::

## 10. Reply to an injunction application

**Rests on:** the defensive architecture of Order XXXIX and Section 41, Specific Relief Act, 1963
**Order of argument:** jurisdiction → statutory bar → procedural defects → the three principles → conduct

> **REPLY ON BEHALF OF THE RESPONDENT/DEFENDANT TO I.A. No. ____ of 20__**
>
> **PRELIMINARY OBJECTIONS**
>
> **1. Jurisdiction.** The jurisdiction of the civil court to entertain the suit is barred by Section ____ of the ____ Act, which constitutes a complete code and provides an exclusive forum and remedy. The application must fail with the suit.
>
> **2. The injunction is barred by statute.** The injunction sought is one which cannot be granted, being barred by Section 41(____) of the Specific Relief Act, 1963, in that ____. This objection is anterior to any question of prima facie case or balance of convenience.
>
> **3. Non-compliance with the proviso to Order XXXIX Rule 3.** The ad interim order dated ____ was granted without notice to the Respondent. This Hon'ble Court did not record its reasons for the opinion that the object of granting the injunction would be defeated by delay, as the proviso to Rule 3 mandatorily requires. Further, the Applicant has failed to file the affidavit of compliance required by clause (b) of the said proviso, on the day of the order or the day immediately following, and has failed to serve the Respondent with the documents required by clause (a). The order is on that ground alone unsustainable.
>
> **4. Non-compliance with Order XXXIX Rule 3A.** More than thirty days have elapsed since the grant of the ad interim order, and the application has neither been finally disposed of nor has this Hon'ble Court recorded its reasons for the inability so to do.
>
> **5. Suppression of material facts.** In the affidavit filed in support of the said application the Applicant has knowingly made false and misleading statements in relation to material particulars, in that the Applicant: (i) suppressed the order dated ____ passed by the ____ Court in Case No. ____, a copy whereof is annexed as **Annexure R-1**, which order proceeds upon a finding that the Respondent was in possession of the suit property; and (ii) asserted in paragraph ____ that ____, which assertion is false to the Applicant's knowledge, as appears from ____. The injunction having been granted without notice to the Respondent, this Hon'ble Court is bound by the **first proviso to Order XXXIX Rule 4** to vacate the same.
>
> **ON THE MERITS**
>
> **6. No prima facie case.** *[Attack the foundation of the claimed right, from the Applicant's own documents.]*
>
> **7. Balance of convenience.** The balance of convenience lies wholly against the grant of an injunction, in that ____. The Respondent has expended ₹____ upon the suit property in reliance upon a sanctioned plan dated ____, and the Applicant stood by and watched from ____ until ____ without protest.
>
> **8. No irreparable injury.** The injury apprehended by the Applicant, if any, is capable of being compensated in money and is therefore not irreparable. The Respondent is a solvent entity with assets of ₹____ and is fully able to satisfy any decree that may be passed.
>
> **9. Delay and acquiescence.** The Applicant has approached this Hon'ble Court after a delay of ____ months from the date on which he admittedly acquired knowledge of the acts complained of, as pleaded in paragraph ____ of his own application. He who seeks equity must come promptly; delay defeats equity.
>
> **10. Lis pendens** *(where the apprehended act is an alienation)***.** In any event, by virtue of Section 52 of the Transfer of Property Act, 1882, any transfer effected during the pendency of the suit will bind the transferee to the result thereof, and the Applicant requires no injunction for his protection.
>
> **PRAYER**
>
> It is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to vacate the ad interim order dated ____, to dismiss I.A. No. ____ of 20__ with exemplary costs, and to pass such other orders as may be just.

::: danger Verify before you file
Every draft on this page is a skeleton. Court fee, valuation, the form of verification, the form of a caveat, and the practice on ex parte injunctions are all governed by **State amendments, State Court Fees Acts and High Court Rules**, which differ materially between jurisdictions. Limitation articles must be checked against the Schedule to the Limitation Act, 1963 for the particular relief claimed. Whether the matter is a **commercial dispute of a Specified Value** changes the applicable regime — Order VI Rule 15A, Order VIII Rule 3A, and the absolute 120-day bar. Nothing here is a substitute for reading the current bare Act and the Rules of the court in which you are filing.
:::
