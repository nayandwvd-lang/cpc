# Find a provision

Three ways in: by **what you are trying to do**, by **section number**, or by **Order**. Every link lands on the provision itself, not the top of a chapter.

::: tip Faster still
Press <kbd>/</kbd> anywhere on the site to search the full text of every provision offline. This page is for when you know roughly what you want but not where it lives.
:::
